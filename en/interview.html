From the Rotoplo Fires to Codéine: what is behind the dates.

Saint-Erblon • Mayenne — band archives

The Twenty Years Interview

Questions asked to the old ones, and the answers the archives light up.

For the launch of the archives online, the team got the boxes out again: the CD case Jar (1998), the posters, the 1993 photos, and the history written at the time. The four from the start and the pillars of the band — Régis, Tanguy, Tony, Loran, Gilles, Jean-Jean and Florent — agreed to follow the thread back.

At the start, four kids and no instrument. How does it begin?

At the start, a quartet “les rotoplo fires”, which makes up a third of the base of Jar. Tanguy on drums — with the sound of a wooden cassette box hit with energy —, Loran singer and lady-killer hero to roll on the ground for, Régis on electric guitar — with one string — and Tony on the smooby synth. In short, everything to succeed...

Tanguy, founder

And there were never any “normal” instruments at the start?

Not in the proper sense, no. We were based in Saint-Erblon, in Mayenne, not far from Rennes and Angers, with the French alternative wave as soundtrack — berrus, Parabellum, OTH... We would soon change name, naturally favouring songs with words: it would be Desastrax. Time to record a four-track cassette on a tape recorder of the time.

Tony, founder

The basement, what was it exactly?

Our place. The basement of the family house, in Saint-Erblon. The amps downstairs, the parents in front of the television upstairs. Everything went back up through the floor. It is this raw acoustics that we still hear on the recordings — and it is there that the sound of the band made itself alone.

Régis, founder
1991

1991, the band starts again. What changes?

We met from time to time to play, as best we could — the start of the struggle — until the arrival of Gilles, who goes straight into the bass. Régis moves to drums, Loran to guitar, Tony to vocals: the progress shows. “Explicit” was born, passing through Soul Famous Carpet.

Tony, founder
1993

First concert: Château-Gontier, 2 September 1993. Do you remember it?

The rock explosion of the West, Angers especially, pushed us to create fast. The songs went by, Jérôme had joined the quartet, and the influences multiplied: Fugazi, Sonic Youth, les Thugs, les Skippies... and the unavoidable Nirvana. The concert was followed by a good performance — which, looking back, made Explicit something else: No Mug.

Loran, founder
1996

Then Helium, in 1996. The hardcore turn?

Yes: the band takes the line of American hardcore fusion, with the Dischord filiation — Fugazi, Quicksand — and all the grunge that was coming in. The guitars got harder, the tempo got tighter.

Loran, founder
1998

1998 : Jar, the CD. How do you record a disc when you rehearse in a basement?

Jérôme leaves the line-up and makes room for Jean-Marie and Florent — that is when the band reaches its peak and records a five-track CD in studio. Five tracks: Little Shit, Endfeels, Quiet, Self Made Women, Shelter for Anger. Recorded at Nono's, at the Panoramix studio, in Saint-Sauveur-de-Flée; the rehearsals happened at Les Courans, in Longuefuye.

Tony, founder
1999

End of 1999, everything stops. Two years of silence.

The band splits and stops all activity for two years. Some, missing music, then join other bands on the side — Jean-Marie joins Hippy Groovy and the Stylee Mens. It was not an end: just a pause we had not admitted to ourselves.

Régis, founder
2002

2002 : Codéine, without Tony. How does it go?

We do it again without Tony, on new influences — Incubus, Sevendust — and with a new one on vocals: Jean-François (jef). We also set up an association, Tous Azic'Muts, with Hippy Groovy: music, but also a structure to defend it.

Gilles, guitarist
2004

And the last straight line, until 2004?

December 2003: it is Gilles who has to leave the band, for work reasons. The rest keeps going, with a more metal style and Florent moving to seven strings. We still play in Rennes, at Campus Kerlan, in January 2004, then in Saint-Michel-de-la-Röe for Aperoconcert, in September. Today the story goes on with more or less empty periods, at the rhythm of everyone's work situation...

Jean-Jean president of the Tous Azic'Muts association
2004

2004 : after Jef leaves, what becomes of the band?

After Jean-François left, Alex arrived on vocals and guitar. The band took the name Wöw, with Régis, Jean-Marie (Jean-Jean) and Florent, the goal being to take a more professional direction. They went on doing concerts in the region, until the band disappears in 2012.

Florent, guitarist

THE BASEMENT

The place, the legend, and the floor that never insulated anything.

THE AMPS DOWNSTAIRS, THE PARENTS UPSTAIRS

The rehearsals took place in the basement of the family house, in Saint-Erblon: the amps and the drums downstairs, the parents in front of the television upstairs. The sound went up through the floor — no sound insulation at all, incomparable raw acoustics.

It is this place that shaped the sound of the band, and it stays attached to all the periods: from Desastrax to Jar, passing through Explicit and No Mug.

THE ANECDOTES

What the archives tell between the lines.

The manure heap

Bikers' rally • Saint-Saturnin-du-Limet (53)

Following one of our first concerts, at the Fête de la musique in Pouancé (49), we had been generously invited to do a concert in Saint-Saturnin-du-Limet (53), as part of a bikers' rally — in September, I think. We had been invited by Jérôme's brother, at the time.

On the day of the concert we had arrived in the most total indifference: “half of the people there were more than drunk”, I remember. And to top it all, it was raining — we arrived wet, we had to play with our jackets on.

We asked Jérôme's brother: “Where is the stage?” And there he told us: “Well, you just have to set up there”, on a tarpaulin, right in the middle of the field. We played, but it was terrible.

The bikers took our instruments to play in front of more spectators than us. We suffered that day.

Since then we remember that day with the joke: “we played on the manure heap”.

Régis, drummer

Échemiré, Not even ready

Festival Cavirock • Échemiré (Maine-et-Loire)

Échemiré, in Maine-et-Loire, we were taking part in this rock festival near Angers organised by the Cavirock association. From my memories, Jérôme was ill. Loran, who had not played with us for a long time, was learning the new songs as best he could in the car on the way. We were before the last band or maybe before, I do not remember too well. It goes back a while.

Finally we arrive; and there, it is the big shock. The bands playing before us were very good technically. Nothing to do with us, the stress went up and the feeling of being completely lost took hold of us.

At the moment of playing, Loran said: “guys, let's go all in” and we started to play and to free ourselves completely. We were jumping everywhere, and there, curiously, the audience started to enjoy it. Loran threw his guitar, held back by its jack cable. It made a return trip above the audience to come back on the stage floor. The audience was stunned. A very good memory.

Tony, singer

One plectrum too many

Explicit • The parents' garage, New Year's Day

We had planned to do a concert for friends in the parents' garage, on the evening of New Year's Day. It was during Explicit, maybe in 91-92, Gilles will be able to remind us.

As usual, the stress of bands that play faster in concert than in rehearsal. We started the concert full on, even faster than usual, I think.

Gilles, our bass player, was suffering with his plectrum, but kept playing until the fateful moment when the tendon gave way. But most of the players had not even noticed and kept playing.

Meanwhile Gilles, sitting on a stool, the bass on his thighs, was trying, as best he could, to lift that thumb that did not want to anymore. I remember trying to lift it with his other hand, but nothing worked. The tendon was well cut. And me, too taken by the music, I was asking him to continue ...

Gilles had to go to a specialist. I remember his hand with a kind of splint, held with rubber bands. A kind of thumb pulley.

We laughed a lot about it. But, honestly, I do not think we would have liked to be in his place.

The positive side, still: after the rehabilitation, Gilles played the bass differently. Pop, slap (Red Hot influence).

He had acquired a remarkable technique, envied by the other bands.

Loran, guitarist

Fête de la musique in Le Mans

Jar • Fête de la musique, Le Mans (72)

In the Jar days, Mickaël had organised a concert for us with a stage dedicated to us. We had to bring the PA in the cars as well as the instruments of course. We were very excited at the idea of presenting our music in a big city of the West. It was finally the moment for us to express our music. Besides, the drinks and snacks were offered by the bar. It smelled of top organisation, not like the ones we had met before.

At last ... After a frustration linked to a missing XLR cable on a PA speaker, we were finally ready. Unfortunately, it was the total flop, not a cat; the famous organising bar was located at Le Mans station. When you know Le Mans, the Fête de la musique is organised in the town centre, not at the station. We had badly digested the adventure. Unfortunately, that is part of the life of a band.

A concert on New Year's Day, oh never

New Year's Eve party • Maine-et-Loire, around 1995-1996

We were invited to a village hall for New Year's Eve, in a village of Maine-et-Loire, not very far from Noëllet. Jérôme was still with us, I would say around 1995-96. The evening had already started well. Tables with white paper tablecloths were laid out facing the stage.

So we started to play. And there, it was the most total disarray. Not an indifference, as we had known on the “manure heap”, but a total rejection of what we were doing. Basically the music did not please. The young people there were not there to listen to a concert but to party and dance. We had whistles, we received food, it seems.

We had entered the cliché of flying tomatoes. We had to react and we reacted. First, by using the feedback full on so as to burst their ears, until we ourselves threw food within reach at the audience. We were wounded inside.

I even saw beer bottles passing from the back of the stage and thrown at the people, not happy to see us there that day. We even learned that a friend, Jean-Marie, who had made the trip from Château-Gontier to see us play, felt a beer bottle fly 5 cm from his head.

A memorable event.

Two years of silence

1999 • 2002

At the end of 1999 the band splits and stops all activity for two years. Some join other line-ups on the side — then in early 2002 everyone does it again under the name Codéine.

THE NAMES OF THE BAND

In order, with the years the archives allow to date.

Before 1988

Les Rotoplo Fires

The original quartet, with no “normal” instruments: one-string guitar, wooden cassette box, smooby synth.

1988

Desastrax

Songs with words, a four-track cassette on a tape recorder of the time — then the dissolution.

Late 80s

Soul Famous Carpet

The passing name, before Explicit takes over.

1991

Explicit

The return: Gilles on bass, Régis on drums, Loran on guitar, Tony on vocals.

1993

No Mug

First concert in Château-Gontier on 2 September 1993; Jérôme joins the line-up.

1996

Helium

American hardcore fusion, inherited from Fugazi, Quicksand and grunge.

1998

Jar

Jean-Marie and Florent join, the band reaches its peak and records its 5-track CD.

2002

Codeine

Without Tony, with Jean-François (Jef) on vocals, and the Tous Azic'Muts association.

2004

Wöw

Jean-François leaves, Alex arrives on vocals and guitar: the band takes the name Wöw, with Régis, Jean-Marie and Florent, and aims at a more professional direction. It still plays in the region until 2012.

2022

Crumble Head

The name we gave ourselves after an evening, for a rebuild of the band — Régis, Loran, Gilles and Tony. Songs started to be born in 2022, like that, for the pleasure, then a few others: nothing concrete for the moment.
