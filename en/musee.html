THE MUSEUM

The objects that followed the band.

The original quartet, with no “normal” instruments: one-string guitar, wooden cassette box, smooby synth. In short, everything to succeed... What survived all that is exhibited here, photo by photo.

THE MUSEUM IS GROWING

The display cases are not all filled.

The Tama drum kit, the amps from the basement, the Desastrax cassettes, the posters, the covers: there are still pieces in the boxes. If you kept an object from this story, or its photo, it has its place in the museum.
