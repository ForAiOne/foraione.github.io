WHAT THEY DO TODAY

Those who held the instruments, and what they have become.

Tanguy
Vocals — Desastrax, at the start

Today a graphic designer and comic book writer. He has signed Vaudou Dou Wap (2004, with Benjamin Bozonnet), La Mue (2007, with Léo Quiévreux), Les Grands Peintres (2015) and Texto Maudit (2018). He also runs L'Écho Sonore, a column about sound, in the magazine Aaarg !, and contributes to the Gazette du Rock of Liège.

Author page — Opale BD Biography and bibliography — Éditions Jungle
Loran
Guitar — Desastrax, at the start

Concert photographer — his lens has followed international and French bands of the rock scene, notably U2, les Wampas, Yann Tiersen and the Ramones, with an exhibition entitled Tohu-Bohu. He is also co-founder, with Yoann Pouteau, of the punk and comic fanzine Bullshit'n'roll (since 2015) and of the festival Spank that goes with it.

Portrait of the fanzine Bullshit'n'roll — Tranzistor The fanzine in the catalogue — Le Fanzinarium
Alex
Vocals and guitar — Wöw (2004-2012)

Arrived on vocals and guitar after Jean-François left, he is today the singer of the band from Laval Octane — rock metal, more than ten years on stage and more than 150 concerts.
